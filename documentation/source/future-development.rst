===================
Future development
===================

The following areas are ideas from the current maintainers, hence, not
commitments or a delivery schedule. Progress may take time because available
maintenance and development time is limited.

Decision support
----------------
Extend the tool to groundwater and other or multiple drinking-water sources,
considering existing groundwater tools and split or parallel trains. Add
raw-water-based recommendations linked to desired removal efficiencies,
decision-tree visualisation, and exclusion filtering.

Optimisation
------------
Broaden process coverage, make the optimiser optional through lazy imports,
separate core DST functionality from optimisation, and standardise on one
calculation engine. Explore adaptability as an objective and compare designs
on a consistent, user-verifiable basis.

Validation and uncertainty
--------------------------
Benchmark against operating plants and expert knowledge, explore
site-specific calibration, and test with external users before wider rollout.
Integrate T6.2 scenarios and quantitative uncertainty analysis (D6.2,
Section 3.5).

Usability and presentation
--------------------------
Improve workflow, in-tool explanations and training; state the expert-user
scope and responsibility for inputs, collect delivery preferences, and show
each treatment step's KPI contribution in an interactive dashboard.

Production readiness
--------------------
Resolve remaining test failures, provide a multi-user database server, secure
application settings, and explore and implement suitable optimiser process
and status management before hosted deployment.

KPI scope and content
---------------------
Clarify gross and net footprint and storage allowances; extend the catalogue
and cost functions; update prices and emission factors; and research
consistent energy and CO2 boundaries, including indirect chemical and media
impacts and Scope 2/3 emission calculations and factors.
