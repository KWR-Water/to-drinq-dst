KPI evaluation

The tool evaluates a treatment plant on two levels. First, each technology in the train gets its own KPIs. Second, the technology KPIs are combined with the design parameters into plant KPIs.

Calculation pipeline

The pipeline is _run_calculation_pipeline in drinq/views.py. It starts when the user presses Calculate on the technology properties page. It reads all inputs from the database, so it always uses the values that were just saved.

        flowchart TD
    A[Design parameters] --> N[Nominal flow]
    B[Technology properties<br/>defaults and user values] --> T[Technology KPIs]
    C[Water quality in and target] --> T
    D[Chemical prices] --> T
    N --> T
    T --> S[Sum over technologies]
    S --> P[Plant KPIs]
    N --> P
    A --> P
    T --> CC[Construction costs]
    CC --> P
    P --> R[Stored plant properties]
    T --> R2[Stored technology KPI values]

Input values

Inputs come from three sources.

  1. Database defaults. TechnologyPropertyJunction.default_value, DesignParameter.defaultvalue and the default chemical prices.

  2. User input. Values entered per plant on the parameter and technology pages.

  3. Calculated values. Properties flagged is_digested are calculated by the KPI classes. Values returned by the optimiser are marked as optimised.

Every stored technology property value carries a source: user, digested or optimized.

Nominal flow

The plant is designed for the nominal flow \(Q\) in m3/h:

\[Q = \left(Q_{pop} + \frac{Q_{large}}{24}\right) (1 + f_{leak}) \, f_{peak}\]

Here \(Q_{pop}\) is the demand of the population in m3/h, \(Q_{large}\) the large-scale demand in m3/day, \(f_{leak}\) the leakage factor and \(f_{peak}\) the peak factor.

Technology KPIs

Each technology is matched on its stage and name to a KPI class in drinq/utils/treatmentkpi.py. The classes share the base class TreatmentKPI and calculate the same set of KPIs.

The available classes cover these groups. Technologies without a class get no technology KPIs.

  • Screening: strainer, micro strainer.

  • Coagulation and flocculation: dynamic mixing, inline mixing, cascade mixer, flocculation.

  • Floc removal: sludge blanket, chain-scraped sedimentation, flotation, lamella clarifier (sloped and horizontal).

  • Filtration: rapid sand (single, dual, pressurised, moving bed), membranes.

  • Advanced treatment: GAC, PAC, UV/H2O2, ozone contactor, ion exchange, pellet softening.

  • Disinfection and storage: chlorine, chloramine, UV, clean water storage.

Plant KPIs

The technology KPIs for footprint, energy, power, head loss, CO2 and chemical costs are summed over the technologies. The plant KPIs are then derived.

Costs

Construction costs. For each technology the cost is a polynomial in one technology property \(x_t\) (for example a volume or an area) or one plant property \(x_p\) (for example the nominal flow):

\[C_{tech} = a x_t^2 + b x_t + c x_p^2 + d x_p + e\]

The coefficients are stored per technology in TechnologyConstructionCostParameter. Unused coefficients are zero. The sum over all technologies is multiplied by surcharge factors for construction (20%), tail costs (5%) and general facilities (5%). The calculation is construction_costs_total in drinq/utils/financial_costs.py.

Annual costs. With construction costs \(C\), lifespan \(L\), interest rate \(i\) and O&M rate \(p\):

\[T = \frac{C}{L} + C\,i + C\,p + E + P + K\]

where \(E\) is the energy costs (daily energy times 365 times the energy price), \(P\) the personnel costs and \(K\) the chemical costs (daily chemical costs times 365). The depreciation is linear and the interest is charged on the full construction costs.

Levelized cost of water.

\[LCOW = \frac{T}{24 \cdot 365 \cdot Q}\]

Adaptability

Adaptability describes how well a technology copes with change, for example in raw water quality. Each technology has a score per AdaptabilityCriterion in TechnologyAdaptabilityScore. The technology score is the mean over its criteria. The plant score is the mean over the technologies and is stored as the plant property adaptability score (0 to 1). The details are shown at /treatmentplant/<id>/adaptability/.

Optimisation

For selected processes the user can run an optimisation instead of a plain calculation. The processes are coagulation-flocculation, single and dual filtration, GAC, chlorination and ozonation.

        flowchart LR
    A[Plant inputs] --> B[Payload and CSV inputs<br/>research/pystimela_optim/inputs]
    B --> C[Config JSON per process]
    C --> D[pystimela_optim run]
    D --> E[results JSON<br/>in outputs]
    E --> F[Store properties,<br/>water quality and KPIs]
    F --> G[Recalculate plant KPIs]

  • The mapping between DRINQ properties and optimiser parameters is stored in TechnologyPystimelaoptJunction, TechnologyPropertyPystimelaoptJunction and TechnologyWaterqualityParameterPystimelaoptJunction. Only properties flagged sent_to_optimizer are sent.

  • By default the run starts in a background thread (setting OPTIMIZER_ASYNC). The page polls /treatmentplant/<id>/technology/optimizer-status/.

  • TreatmentPlantTechnologyOptimizationStatus records per plant and technology whether the result is current. It stores a hash of the inputs. When the user changes an input, the hash no longer matches and the stale result is dropped.

  • Reset optimization clears the flags and recalculates with the user and default values.

Comparing designs

/treatmentplant/compare/ places the plant properties of two designs side by side per category. The radar chart scales each property to the larger of the two values and shows the mean per category.
