Glossary

Key definitions and abbreviations

Adaptability

The ability of a technology to cope with changing conditions. Scored per criterion and averaged.

CAPEX

Capital expenditure. In the tool, the construction costs.

Closure table

A table with all ancestor and descendant pairs of a tree. Used for the decision tree.

Deciding factor

A node of the decision tree that holds a question or a choice.

Design tree

The decision tree of one water source.

Digested property

A technology property that is calculated by the tool instead of entered by the user.

GAC

Granular activated carbon.

KPI

Key performance indicator, for example energy use per m3.

LCOW

Levelized cost of water. Total annual costs per m3 of water produced.

Nominal flow

The design flow of the plant in m3/h.

O&M

Operation and maintenance.

OMP

Organic micropollutant.

Stage

A step in a treatment train, such as screening, coagulation or disinfection.

Treatment plant

A named treatment train with its design parameters and results.

Treatment train

An ordered sequence of treatment technologies.
