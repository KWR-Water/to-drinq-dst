Welcome to the design support tool

This tool supports you in designing small-scale drinking water treatment plants. It guides you through a series of steps, from naming your design, selecting a water source, answering additional questions, to finally showing you a recommended configuration for your treatment plant.

Getting started

The design process constitutes of a series of steps:

    {% if not user.is_authenticated %}
  • Register and login to the tool.
    • {% endif %}
  • Create a new treatment plant by providing a name and description.
  • Answer a questionnaire to determine the most suitable treatment configuration for the treatment plant.
  • Define the treatment plant by selecting a configuration ('treatment option') and setting the design parameters.
  • View the results of the design, i.e. the performance indicators.

You can start a new design by clicking on "Start a new design" in the menu above. If you have previously saved a configuration of treatment design options, you can load it by clicking on "Load a stored configuration session".

About

This tool has been developed in the context of the ToDrinQ project, which aims at developing a toolkit of technologies, systems and decision tools that will improve overall water systems operations and secure high quality drinking water. The project is funded by the European Union and involves a consortium of research institutes and companies.
